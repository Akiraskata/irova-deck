IROVA Studio — Business

Building worlds
around color.

IROVA Studio is building a new character and lifestyle ecosystem where color connects digital devices, fashion, everyday objects and original characters.

The Concept

Color is
the starting point.

People already choose the colors they live with. IROVA turns those colors into characters, products and worlds that can become part of everyday life.

Business Model

From color
to ecosystem.

IROVA connects original characters with physical products, creating multiple entry points and long-term product relationships.

01

Color

A familiar color becomes the visual anchor for the entire product experience.

02

Character

Each color world is represented by original characters with their own identity and story.

03

Lifestyle

Characters and colors extend into accessories, fashion, bags, collectibles and everyday goods.

Color Worlds

Three worlds.
One ecosystem.

IROVA begins with distinct color worlds, each carrying its own atmosphere, character identity and product direction.

Olive

Nature, calmness and everyday outdoor life.

Canyon

Warm landscapes, exploration and movement.

Purple

Night cities, digital culture and imagination.
